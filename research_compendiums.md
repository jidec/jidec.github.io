# Research Compendiums

Associated with each of my first-authored papers since 2024 is a [research compendium](https://research-compendium.science/) built as a [Quarto Website](https://quarto.org/docs/websites/).

Each compendium contains literate code documenting all the steps required to reproduce my results and figures. 

These research products are a manifestation of my commitment to reproducibility, accessibility, and effeciency in science. 

Using my [final template](https://github.com/jidec/research-compendium-template) from 2026:

	[carabid-color-macroeco](https://jidec.github.io/carabid-color-macroeco)

	[nearctic-odonate-wings-analysis](https://jidec.github.io/nearctic-odonate-wings-analysis/): Interactions between sexual signaling and wing size drive ecology and evolution of wing colors in Odonata (2024)
